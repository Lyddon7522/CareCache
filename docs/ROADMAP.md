# Roadmap

## Foundation

- Brand, architecture, Drift schema, localization, adaptive navigation, and CI.
- Overview, Inventory, Schedule, Devices, and Settings foundations.
- Local backup/restore and public legal pages.

## MVP completeness

- Barcode quick-add/quick-use and unknown barcode flow.
- Physical-device barcode benchmark across UPC/EAN/DataMatrix labels that are small, curved,
  damaged, low contrast, or poorly lit.
- Label OCR suggestions with confirmation.
- Reliable notifications across restarts, time-zone changes, and denied permissions.
- Device and CPAP reference-settings editor.
- Empty, error, permission, and restore states.
- Android and iOS integration journeys and accessibility pass.

## Beta

- Inventory lots, packages, expiration warnings, and estimated run-out.
- Local care profiles and travel kits.
- Prescription-setting history and document attachments.
- Encrypted backup option and local app lock.
- Store metadata, screenshots, signing, TestFlight, and Play Internal distribution.

## Later, only with explicit opt-in

- Public barcode catalog lookup.
- Recall alerts.
- User-controlled peer-to-peer or cloud synchronization without a Vector42 account.
